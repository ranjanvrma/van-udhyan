A living atlas of Bavdhan's urban forest.

Van Udyan is a conservation project run by RSWF. Volunteers plant, monitor and record every tree, flower and bird we find — building a public, open record of the biodiversity that calls this hillside home.

What we're building

Van Udyan started as a reforestation effort on a bare hillside in Bavdhan. Today it holds dozens of planted species, hundreds of recorded wild sightings, and three monitoring zones that let RSWF see how the forest is changing — season by season, year by year.
